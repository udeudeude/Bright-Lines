Tracking and calibration

Original Pictionary Air pens illuminate green while the blue button is held. This experimental tracker finds the strongest green-dominant luminous cluster in each camera frame. Keep other green lights out of view, use even lighting, and move slowly at first. The marker shows what the camera detects. Strokes begin when the light appears and end when it disappears. Detection is experimental and not yet verified against your pen.

If tracking is noisy, increase Threshold. If the pen disappears, decrease it. Try both cameras. iOS requires HTTPS (or localhost) for camera permission. SVG exports are editable vector paths.
